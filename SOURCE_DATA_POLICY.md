# Source-data policy

No patient-level data are included in this release. Download public inputs from their originating repositories under the relevant terms of use. Do not upload controlled, personally identifiable, or institutionally restricted material.

The manuscript-level source inventory identifies GEO, BioSino/NODE OEP001105 and related public accessions. OEP001105 is hosted through BioSino/NODE; it is not a ProteomeXchange accession. The delivered figures are regenerated from frozen, accession-traceable result tables rather than redistributed source matrices.

Before a public archive is created, add a machine-readable accession table, file checksums for all redistributable derived tables, the archive DOI, and the repository tag/commit to the manuscript data- and code-availability statements.
